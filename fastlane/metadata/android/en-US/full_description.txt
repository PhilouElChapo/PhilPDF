PhilPDF is a free and open-source PDF editor: edit, sign, fill and scan your documents right on your phone. No subscription, no ads, no account to create and no trackers.

<b>✏️ Edit your PDFs</b>
<ul>
<li>Edit text while keeping the original font</li>
<li>Highlight, underline, strike out, notes, drawings, arrows, shapes, images and stamps</li>
<li>True redaction: hidden text is really removed, not just covered</li>
<li>Watermark, header and footer</li>
</ul>

<b>🖊️ Fill and sign</b>
<ul>
<li>Fill PDF forms, add fields, checkboxes and lists</li>
<li>Sign by hand or with a digital signature (certificate), and verify the signatures you receive</li>
<li>Signatures and fields can be moved and resized afterwards</li>
<li>Reusable stamps: "Paid", "Received on", your company stamp…</li>
</ul>

<b>📷 Scan and recognize text</b>
<ul>
<li>Scan documents with the camera, several pages at once</li>
<li>Straighten and clean up scans: white background, sharp text, even on security paper</li>
<li>Text recognition (OCR): your scans become searchable and copyable</li>
</ul>

<b>📄 Organize, compress, combine</b>
<ul>
<li>Reorder, rotate, extract and delete pages</li>
<li>Combine several PDFs into one</li>
<li>Compress a PDF that is too big to e-mail</li>
<li>Search the document, bookmarks, reading mode for small screens</li>
<li>Print, share the file or a link</li>
</ul>

<b>☁️ Your clouds, directly</b>
Open and save on Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, pCloud, Infomaniak kDrive and WebDAV, with several accounts and a preferred one. Create folders without leaving the app. Sync with PhilPDF on Windows and Linux: recent files, stamps and signatures follow you.

<b>🤖 Optional AI assistant</b>
With your own Claude or Gemini key: summarize, explain simply, translate a document, fill a form, rename and file, extract invoice data. Nothing is sent without your tap, sensitive data is flagged before sending, and every answer can be reported.

<b>🔒 Your documents stay yours</b>
PhilPDF has no server. Your files stay on your device; clouds and AI are reached only when you ask.

<b>💚 Free software</b>
Open source (AGPL v3), also available on Windows and Linux. Interface in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.
Source code and releases: https://github.com/PhilouElChapo/PhilPDF
