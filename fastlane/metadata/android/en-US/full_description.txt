PhilPDF is a free and open-source PDF reader and editor (AGPL v3), the Android version of PhilPDF for Windows and Linux. Same engine (MuPDF): same rendering, same annotations. No account, no ads, no trackers.

<b>Read</b>
<ul>
<li>Home screen with your recent files and thumbnails</li>
<li>Continuous scrolling, sharp pinch zoom, reading mode for small screens, bookmarks, comments</li>
<li>Password-protected PDFs</li>
</ul>

<b>Edit and sign</b>
<ul>
<li>Highlight, underline, notes, drawings, shapes, arrows, images, stamps</li>
<li>Edit text, redact (text really removed), watermark, header and footer</li>
<li>Fill forms, add form fields, digital signatures and their verification</li>
<li>Organize pages, straighten and clean up scans, text recognition (OCR)</li>
</ul>

<b>Your clouds</b>
Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, pCloud, Infomaniak kDrive and WebDAV, several accounts at once, with a preferred account for sending and reading. Sync with PhilPDF on your PCs: recent files, stamps and signatures.

<b>Optional AI assistant</b>
With your own Claude or Gemini key: summarize, explain simply, translate, fill a form, rename and file, extract data. Nothing is sent without your tap, sensitive data is flagged first, and every AI result can be reported.

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
PhilPDF has no server. Your documents stay on your device; clouds and AI services are reached directly, only when you ask.

Source code and releases: https://github.com/PhilouElChapo/PhilPDF
